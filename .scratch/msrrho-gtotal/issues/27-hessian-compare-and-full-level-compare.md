# 27: `hessian_compare` at the reference geometry, and `level_compare` on every quantity

**What to build:** a `hessian_compare` Calculation (`openqha/thermochem/hessian_compare.py`) that, for every MACE basin the merge map marks `kept`, evaluates the MACE Hessian **at the reference geometry** x_r (`$atoms` of `orca/<level>/basinNN/job.hess`, via `hessian.hessian(atoms, calc, mode="analytic")`, stored as the engine file `mace/basinNN/hessian_at_<level>.npy` together with the MACE residual force there) and compares it with the reference `$hessian`. Both sides are the same point of the same space, so every metric is taken in one basis and no mode assignment is invented. Four families, as the MLIP-Hessian literature reports them:

1. **Element-wise, Cartesian** (HIP, PFT, Rodriguez, PHL): `HESS_MAE_EV_A2`, `HESS_RMSE_EV_A2` over the 3N x 3N raw Hessians.
2. **Basis-free, projected**: on `K = P M^-1/2 H M^-1/2 P` (Eckart projector P, both sides through `mode_match.projected_modes` / `project_and_diagonalise`): `HESS_REL_FROBENIUS = ||K_m - K_r||_F / ||K_r||_F`.
3. **Spectrum, sorted index** (Weyl's inequality makes sorted pairing the bound-preserving one; it is what every frequency-MAE in the literature does): `EIG_MAE`, `FREQ_MAE_CM`, `FREQ_MAX_CM` over all modes and `FREQ_MAE_LOW_CM`, `FREQ_MAX_LOW_CM`, `N_LOW` over modes < 300 cm^-1; `SOFTENING_SLOPE` = least-squares s in omega_m = s * omega_r through the origin (Deng et al. 2025), all modes and low modes; `LOWEST_ENGINE_CM`, `LOWEST_REF_CM`, `N_IMAGINARY_ENGINE_AT_REF`.
4. **Mode-resolved on the reference eigenbasis** (no pairing at all): `D = L_r^T K_m L_r`. Its diagonal is the MACE curvature along each DFT normal mode -- the Hessian-vector product PHL trains on, with the DFT modes as deterministic probe vectors -- giving `OMEGA_ALONG_REF_CM[i] = sqrt(D_ii)` per mode and `MIXING = ||D - diag(D)||_F / ||D||_F`. Eigenvector agreement as HIP reports it, block-aware: `overlap_matrix(L_r, L_m)` summed over `degenerate_blocks(omega_r, 20 cm^-1)` -> `MIN_BLOCK_OVERLAP`, plus the sorted-index cosine `|v_i^m . v_i^r|` for the record. `mode_match.match` and `hybrid_spectrum` are NOT used: their argmax pairing with counted collisions was built for the covariance-vs-Hessian problem where the two bases come from different estimators; here they are exact eigenbases of two matrices at one point.

The names and definitions follow HIP's `scripts/eval_horm.py` (`hessian-train/hip-main`) so the numbers read against its tables: `HESSIAN_MAE` (= `hessian_mae`), `EIGVAL_MAE_ECKART`, `EIGVAL1_MAE_ECKART`, `EIGVEC1_COS_ECKART`, `EIGVEC2_COS_ECKART` (lowest two modes, sorted index, |dot|), `EIGVEC_OVERLAP_ERROR = ||abs(Q_m^T Q_r) - I||_F`, `NEG_NUM_AGREE` (imaginary counts agree), `ASYMMETRY_MAE` (0 for an AD Hessian; recorded). Frequency MAE at sorted index is the IR-benchmark convention (arXiv 2605.22367, MACE 3.2 cm^-1 on 24 molecules) and is what `FREQ_MAE_CM` reports.

Curvature expressed in the units the project cares about, still at x_r and still a Hessian metric: the `crest`-preset per-mode T*S on omega_r and on `OMEGA_ALONG_REF_CM` -> `TS_LOW_DELTA` (sum over modes < 300), and `S_VIB_CURVATURE_DELTA`, `ZPE_CURVATURE_DELTA` (HIP's ZPE error, PFT's S MAE). The residual MACE force at x_r (`MAX_FORCE_ENGINE_AT_REF_EV_A`) is recorded because the projected Hessian at a non-stationary point is the standard treatment but not an exact one.

Thermochemistry proper is compared **at each level's own geometry** only: the two levels' `[[Basin]]` rows side by side (E_REL, ZPE, H_THERMAL, S_VIB, S_ROT, G_I, POPULATION), paired by the merge map, and `[Ensemble]` / `[Result]` terms (S_REF, S_CONF_PRIME, DS_BAR, H_CONF, CP_CONF, S_ABS, G_TOTAL, max |dp_i|). `$dipole_derivatives` compared only where both engines have them; MACE-OFF23 has no charges: `DIPOLE_DERIVATIVES_PRESENT = false`. Records `levels/hessian_compare.out/.toml` with `[Calculation_Info]` (ENGINE_LEVEL, REFERENCE_LEVEL, GEOMETRY = "reference", PRESET, N_BASINS_COMPARED, N_MERGED, N_SADDLE, DIPOLE_DERIVATIVES_PRESENT), `[[Basin]]`, `[Ensemble]`; the Report prints per basin the low-mode table (omega_r, omega_m sorted, omega along the reference mode, block overlap, T*S both). `level_compare` grows: `[[Level]]` adds S_REF, DS_BAR, H_CONF, CP_CONF, N_BASINS_90; `[Tiers]` adds MODEL_ERROR_ for each ensemble term; the Report prints the per-basin table. `s0_thermo_msrrho.py --step hessian_compare`.
