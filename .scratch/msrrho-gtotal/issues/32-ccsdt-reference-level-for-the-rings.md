**What the dry run found (2026-09-17, all in `levels/dlpno-ccsdt_cc-pvtz/mode_curvature_dryrun.*` and the scratch runs under `orca/{ri-mp2_cc-pvtz,ri-mp2_aug-cc-pvtz,ccsdt_cc-pvtz,dlpno-ccsdt_cc-pvtz_normalpno}/basin00/`):**

1. *The straight line.* At a 1e-4 Eh rise (delta_q 0.29 on propanal's 131 cm^-1 torsion, hydrogens 0.2-0.3 A) the line is quartic-dominated (E(2)/E(1) = 8-17 instead of 4; wB97M's own quartic is 87 % of the harmonic rise at k = 1) and the 5-point curvature scattered HF 271 / RI-MP2 107 / DLPNO ~0. At 1e-5 (delta_q 0.093) wB97M converges (140.7 at delta_q, 140.8 at delta_q/2) but DLPNO's PNO noise takes over. `TARGET_RISE_EH` is now 1e-5 and the record carries `DELTA_CONVERGENCE_CM` (5-point at delta_q vs delta_q/2) as the error bar, `FD_ERROR_CM` (5-point vs 3-point) as the anharmonicity of the line only.
2. *The gradient term.* RI-MP2/cc-pVTZ at its own minimum: 136.2 cm^-1 (experiment 135.1, mm-wave, v = 2 at 269.3); the same level at the wB97M geometry, 0.009 A RMSD away (C=O +0.011 A): 92.7 (NumFreq), 98.6 (D_ii along the wB97M mode), 107 (line); aug-cc-pVTZ 62 / 74. Canonical CCSD(T)/cc-pVTZ at the wB97M geometry: -65 cm^-1, converged in delta_q (5-point -64.9 at both steps), HF alone +265. A straight displacement along a torsion lengthens C=O at second order (dr ~ d^2/2r); a level whose C=O wants to be longer than wB97M's gains g dr, the same order as the harmonic rise. The Cartesian Hessian of a level at a foreign geometry is a well-defined quantity (it is what Hessian learning trains on) but it is not that level's harmonic frequency. MACE at x_r vs at its own minimum: oxetane 82.6 / 93.1, 2-methyloxirane 190.2 / 193.5, cyclopropanol 235.0 / 263.4, propanal 129.4 / 128.5 -- `hessian_compare`'s at-x_r low-mode numbers contain the same term.
3. *The reference's own label noise.* wB97M-D3(BJ)/def2-TZVPPD analytic Hessian at the same geometry: 131.3 (DefGrid2, the production setting), 137.2 (DefGrid3), 131.45 (NoCOSX); the finite-difference curvature of its energies: 140.8. The default grid puts ~10 cm^-1 on the softest mode; DefGrid3 halves it at 2.1x the cost (8.7 vs 4.1 min).
4. *Oxetane.* DLPNO at the wB97M geometry: E(+-1) - E0 = -6.6e-5 Eh (-14.4 cm^-1), wide profile symmetric; RI-MP2 at the same points -3.2 cm^-1; RI-MP2 TightOpt from x_r lands 0.003 A away, planar, with an imaginary puckering mode (-79.6) and a well ~3 cm^-1 deep along it; wB97M and MACE single wells. The far-IR barrier is 15.5 cm^-1 (fundamental 52.92). Qualitatively CCSD(T) and MP2 see the double well and DFT / MACE do not; the -14.4 carries an unquantified gradient term and is not the barrier.
5. *2-methyloxirane / cyclopropanol* (DLPNO at x_r): 164 +- 19 / garbage (453 +- 340) at the 1e-5 rise; 161 +- 46 / 285 +- 78 at 1e-4; wB97M self-checks 1.0 / 1.6-11.6; MACE 190.5 / 237.
