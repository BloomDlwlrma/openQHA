**What to build:** the production fine-tune of MACE-OFF23_medium on the campaign Dataset's **basin frames**, as ONE row and nothing else for now:

1. **The smoke run** on the tianhe smoke Dataset (the test run: one day of CREST + one day of ORCA Hessians; `04_dataset.py --train-generators basin`): the same recipe as the production row scaled to that set -- Replay = 4 x its `N_TRAIN_HESSIAN` frames at `config_weight = 10`, `w_H` = the epoch-0 balance on its train split (`smoke_fit.epoch_zero_balance`, Cartesian), `cartesian` target, k = 4, the control of ticket 21, a fixed epoch count (20). It proves the machinery end to end on tianhe and its Record gives seconds per epoch, `u`, `g`, the validation share and the peak memory, from which the production row's wall time is written down before it is submitted.
2. **The production row R4** on draw300 (ticket 08's labels, `--train-generators basin`): Replay = **4 x `N_TRAIN_HESSIAN`** frames (the number read from the Dataset Record after 04 runs; drawn with `s0_spice_pt_draw.py --n <4 x N_TRAIN_HESSIAN> --seed 0 --weight 10`, its `.valid.extxyz` passed as `--pt-valid-file`), `config_weight = 10`, `w_H` = the epoch-0 balance on draw300's train split, everything else the defaults of ticket 21 (`--mode-weighting cartesian`, `--probe rademacher --n-probes 4`, lr 0.01, scheduler_patience 20, patience 50, ema, Stage Two at 3/4, `swa_hessian_weight` by the rule), 100 epochs. Its Record prints `PT_N_FRAMES`, `REPLAY_PER_HESSIAN_FRAME` (= 4.0 by construction), both heads' counts, `STAGE_TWO_EPOCH`, `HESSIAN_CURVE_MOVED` and the three validation curves. No `w_H` scan, no R0-R3, no other candidate.
3. **The judge with the gate closed** (S0-C-60; ticket 22): `06_judge.py --engine MACE-OFF23_medium-R4 --spice-file spice_test_5000.extxyz` -- every row reported against its number (the Hessian matrix against the Label vs base; in_distribution; forgetting; the low-mode line; the msRRHO entropy once the msRRHO pipeline has run with this engine; the RMS bins), `VERDICT = REPORTED`; the MD ramp only afterwards, with `--ramp`, if wanted.
4. **The registration**: R4 in `ENGINES` (`--register --register-copy`) with the Dataset index path, the config SHA and `MACE_FORK_COMMIT` (904dd3b or later) as `source`, `params_sha256` pinned; `docs/` gains the campaign page (what was trained on what, the settings, the measured cost from the array logs and the smoke run, the judge table with every row, the paper's sentence per class). What comes after R4 -- a `w_H` scan, the rows R0-R3, reopening the gate with measured thresholds -- is decided on R4's numbers, not before.
